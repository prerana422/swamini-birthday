A letter for my Swamini
To my Swamini, my forever girl ❤️ Seven years of friendship. And honestly, I never imagined that the girl I used to stand with at the school gate, waiting for our moms to come and pick us up, would one day become my best friend forever. Little did I know that those simple school-gate moments would turn into seven years of memories, laughter, endless calls, hangouts, happiness, and a friendship that I would cherish for the rest of my life. Swamini, you are the reason the moments I once thought would be the hardest parts of my year became so much easier to get through. Just having you by my side made everything feel a little less difficult. Our hangouts, our calls, our trauma, our happiness, our endless conversations, and basically sharing every little moment of life together have made everything so much better and more special. You are genuinely one of the best people, Swamini. You're the kind of friend everyone deserves, and I feel so blessed that God chose me to experience a friendship like ours. You always make sure that everyone around you feels special, but gurl, I hope you know that YOU are the most special person in my life. So many things have happened throughout these seven years, but somehow, having each other made everything easier. We've seen so many moments together, and I wouldn't trade any of them for anything. I may not always be the greatest friend like you are, but one thing I can promise is that I love you SO MUCH, gurl. More than I can probably ever put into words. I hope we never stop making memories, having our random conversations, laughing over the smallest things, and being there for each other through every phase of life. Stay by my side forever, gurl. Because seven years down, and I still want a lifetime of this friendship. Happy Birthday, my favourite human. I LOVE YOU A LOT. ❤️ Forever your girl.
♡
